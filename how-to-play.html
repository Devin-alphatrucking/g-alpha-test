How to play DVN Haul

DVN Haul is a free truck delivery game that runs in your browser. Pick a job, load up at the depot, drive to the customer and beat the timer. This guide covers the controls, how stars and coins work, and the tricks that make three-star runs easy.

The truck only steers while it is rolling, like a real vehicle. To turn around on a narrow road, press forward and steer, then reverse and steer the other way.

Your first delivery

  1. Choose a job. Each card shows the customer, how long the route is (Short, Medium or Long), the time limit and the coins on offer.
  2. Drive to the DVN Depot. Follow the yellow ring and arrow. The timer does not start until you pick up the cargo.
  3. Deliver to the customer. Follow the teal ring to their house. Stay on the road: buildings and lawns are solid.
  4. Collect your reward. You earn coins, XP and friendship with the customer, who reacts to how the trip went.

Two or more stars in a row builds a combo: +10% coins for each extra delivery, up to +40%. Crashing breaks your combo.

Traffic, buses and pedestrians

  • Traffic drives on the left, Australian style. Cars slow for you and stop for pedestrians on zebra crossings.
  • Buses are long and slow, and pause at stops before each intersection. Be patient, or take another street.
  • A crash costs you 3 seconds and resets your combo. Hitting a pedestrian costs 2 seconds. Cars can't be driven through, so brake early.

Day, night and weather

A full day passes every five minutes, from golden hour to a dark night. Street lamps and headlights switch on at dusk, and rain and fog roll in. Rain reduces your grip and top speed, so ease off in corners.

Neighbours, stories and friendships

Five neighbours live in town: Granny Maple, Rex the Builder, Pixel, Dr. Whiskers and Captain Nemo. Every delivery builds friendship. When it reaches certain levels a gold STORY job appears: a short scene where your choice changes how they treat you. There are fifteen stories in total, and finishing a neighbour's arc unlocks a special truck paint. Open the Journal to see what each neighbour remembers about you.

Daily quests, upgrades and paint

  • Daily quests: three fresh goals every day, paid automatically when complete.
  • Garage upgrades: Turbo Engine (+12% speed per level) and Better Contracts (+10% coins per level).
  • Paint shop: eight paint jobs. Two are bought with coins, five are unlocked by finishing stories.

Playing with friends

Press Room to create a private room of up to six drivers and share the 4-letter code or invite link. Everyone sees each other's trucks, shares a team goal (20 deliveries for a bonus) and earns +10% coins per friend online. Chat is quick preset emotes only. Only share a room code with people you know.

Tips for three-star runs

  • Start with Short jobs while you learn the road grid.
  • Brake before corners instead of bouncing off the curb.
  • In rain and at night, drive a little slower than you want to.
  • Spend your first coins on the Turbo Engine: it also makes three stars easier.
  • Skip the wait behind a bus by turning down a parallel street.
