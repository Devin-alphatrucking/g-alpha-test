Privacy policy

Last updated: __BUILD_DATE__. DVN Haul is made by Alpha Trucking. This page explains, in plain language, what the game does with information. We keep it minimal on purpose.

Draft for review. This policy describes how the software is built to behave. Have it reviewed by a legal adviser for your jurisdiction before launch, and update it if you switch on ads, analytics or the AI feature.

In short

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, age or location.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device, in your browser.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Players under 13 never see or load ads.

Stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember progress: coins, level, upgrades, friendships and story choices, daily quests, chosen truck paint, sound setting, whether you have seen the tutorial and, only when ads are enabled, whether you said you are under 13 or 13 and over. This never leaves your device unless a feature below says so. You can erase it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Play rooms

If you create or join a room, your browser sends the server a randomly generated nickname (for example "Happy Quokka 42"), your truck's position, truck paint and the number of deliveries made since you joined. Nothing you type is sent, because rooms use preset emotes only. The server also sees your IP address as part of every web request. To limit abuse (for example, creating too many rooms) it keeps a one-way hash of your IP address and a timestamp for up to an hour. Room data is held in temporary storage on the server. A room is removed once it has been inactive for about 30 minutes, and clean-up runs whenever someone creates or joins a room.

AI character lines

When switched on, the neighbours' replies are written by an AI service (Anthropic). The game sends our server only gameplay facts, such as how many deliveries you have made to a character, how often you were late, the weather and your story choices. Names, free text, device identifiers and your IP address are not passed to the AI service. Our server may set a strictly necessary session cookie (PHPSESSID) to rate-limit these requests. It does not identify you and is not used for advertising.

Advertising (off by default)

If we enable ads, the game first asks whether you are under 13. If you choose "under 13", no advertising code is loaded. Players who are 13 or older may choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for bonus coins. Our advertising partner (for example Google) may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, according to its own policies. We configure ads to be non-personalised wherever the partner allows it. If this applies to you, a privacy notice for the advertising partner will be linked here.

Analytics (off by default)

If we enable analytics, we use a privacy-friendly, cookie-less service that reports anonymous, aggregated numbers such as page views, how people arrived and whether they started a game. It does not build profiles of individuals. Analytics are never loaded for players who said they are under 13.

Fonts and third parties

Fonts, images and code are served from this site itself, so visiting does not send your details to a font provider. Links to other websites are not used inside the game.

Children

The game is designed to be suitable for all ages and collects no personal details. See For parents for more.

Your choices and rights

You can clear saved progress at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact below. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your privacy regulator.

Changes

If we change how the game handles information we will update this page and its date.
