DVN Haul for parents

DVN Haul is a gentle, cartoon driving game for all ages. It was built to be safe by design: there are no accounts, no open chat and no collection of personal details. Here is exactly what that means.

The short version

  • No account or sign-up. Players never enter a name, email, age or password to play.
  • No open chat. The only communication is five preset emotes ("Hi!", "GG!", "Wait for me!", "Thanks!", "Let's go!").
  • Generated names. In rooms, players get a random name like "Happy Quokka 42". It is never chosen by the player.
  • Progress stays on the device. Coins, levels and stories are saved only in the browser.
  • Ads are off by default, and when switched on they are optional "watch for bonus coins" breaks that players under 13 never see or load.
  • Gentle content. Cartoon cars can bump, which only costs time. There is no violence, gambling, or in-game purchases with real money.

Playing with friends

Players can create a private room of up to six drivers. A room is only reachable with its 4-letter code or invite link, so please encourage children to share codes only with people they know. Rooms are removed once they have been inactive for about 30 minutes. Because there is no free-text chat, there is no way to send messages, links or images to other players.

The AI characters

The five neighbours react to how you drive. When this feature is switched on, the game sends only gameplay facts (for example "late three times", "delivered in the rain") to our server, which asks an AI service to write one short, family-friendly sentence. Names, free text and other identifying details are never sent, and the characters are instructed to stay in character and never ask for personal information. If the AI is unavailable, built-in lines are used instead.

Ads, spending and screen time

  • There is nothing to buy with real money.
  • When ads are enabled, the game first asks whether the player is under 13. Choosing "under 13" means no ads are ever loaded.
  • A day in the game lasts about five minutes and there are natural stopping points after every delivery. The game pauses when the tab is hidden, and you can pause any time with P or Esc.

Accessibility

The game is fully playable with a keyboard, has an on-screen D-pad for touch screens, respects the "reduce motion" setting (no camera sweeps, shaking or lightning flashes) and has a sound switch.
